# Version 16: The Gym

Nutty asked (2026-10-19): "Add a gym mode that puts you in a gym after the game is in session you're able to walk over to pick up balls to shoot, you can walk to an area where you can spawn a npc on multiple variations of difficulty, squeeky shiny floors, but make it a very fun gym that anyone will spend hours in and lose themselves in".

New title item: **The Gym** (`CFG.mode = 'gym'`, source in `src/e_gym.js`, hooks in c_world, d_game, e_play, f_ui).

- A closed gym: cream block walls over a brick-red band, high windows, exit doors, padded mats behind each backboard, folded bleachers on the far sideline, benches, a water cooler and gym bags on the near one, five felt banners, THE GYM lettering, a wall clock and a live wall scoreboard (your points, the defender's points, the session or challenge clock).
- The floor is varnished: a mirror pass each frame (a second camera under the floor, three.js Reflector style, sampled by projection in a floor shader) plus specular highlights from the three rows of ceiling lights. Graphics Low turns the mirror off. Squeaks: the usual sprint squeak is louder and more frequent, and every hard cut or stop on the floor squeaks too (`Snd.squeak(v)` now takes a volume).
- Free roam, no clock, no fouls, no out of bounds: the walls bounce the ball back (`Snd.thud`). Both baskets are live; the end you carry the ball toward is the one you attack (2K camera flips with it). Session stats in the HUD: FG, streak, best streak (saved record). Heat and on-fire work as usual.
- Balls: two racks on the near sideline, walk into one with empty hands and you have a ball (the rack refills). The game has one physics ball, so the ball you left behind stays on the floor as a stray; walk over any stray to pick it up. The oldest stray is cleared past seven.
- Red pad (far sideline): ✕ spawns a defender at the current level, ◯ cycles Rookie / Pro / All-Star / Legend (Legend is a 97+ with maxed defense, a step faster), △ sends him home, □ brings in or takes out a rebounder (a big who chases misses and feeds you; never controllable). The defender guards whoever has the ball, scores on you if he gets it (HE SCORED ON YOU, his points on the HUD and wall board), and waits at the top of the key while you are off the floor.
- Green pad (near sideline): ✕ 3PT Contest (5 racks of 5 around the arc, the last ball is a money ball worth 2, 60 s), ◯ Around the World (7 spots at 5.1 m, make it to move on, fewest shots and the time), △ Hot Spots (45 s, a random lit spot, 2s and 3s, three in a row adds a point). Shots count only from within 1.6 m of the lit spot; a fresh ball waits at the spot once the last shot resolved. Records save in localStorage `hoopdreams.gym` and show on the pad hint; a new record gets a banner. ◯ on the pad calls a challenge off.
- Setup for the gym: 1 or 2 players (two players share the one ball, pass it around), your player (anyone from the Blacktop pool), the first defender level. Pause works (Restart resets the session, records stay).
- Engine edits: `B.shot.H` remembers the basket a shot was taken at (so running to the other end mid-flight does not lose a make), `giveBall`/`assignDefenders`/`switchPlayer`/icon select skip `gymRole` players, the Blacktop possession clock is gated to Blacktop, `shotP` has no catch-up in the gym, the gym has its own hint line and HUD.

Checks (headless Chromium, swiftshader): boot, title and setup items, 6 of 6 scripted jumpers at both ends (atk flips), rack grab with the loose ball turned into a stray and a stray swap, a ball fired at the wall stays inside, the defender pad at every level (spawn, cycle, replace, send home, tag text, team counts, no leftovers), the rebounder fed the ball back 6 of 6 times and never took control, all three challenges run to the end with records saved, pause / resume / restart / quit, then Play Now, Blacktop and a 2 player gym still run with no errors. With a human-like release spread: 3PT Contest 24 (19 of 25), Hot Spots 33, a sprint dunk at the rim.

Self rating: 8 / 10. Looks and feels like a gym (the mirror floor sells it), plenty to do, records to chase. Known limits: one physics ball for two players, the defender can be a pest right under you when you shoot standing still (make space), the rebounder does not box out.
