# Version 13: Fight mode

Nutty asked (2026-10-19): a fighting mode in the game, inspired by and with the same controls as Marvel vs Capcom, 1v1 only (no tag teams, no assists), with different players having special powers based on Street Fighter, Tekken and Marvel vs Capcom. It is a mode on the main menu of the same game at the same pinned link.

## What shipped

- **Fight** on the main menu, next to Play Now and Blacktop. Setup picks P1 and P2 (or the CPU) from the whole Blacktop pool (every player on the nine teams plus the free agents), 1 player vs CPU or 2 player versus, rounds to win (1, 2 or 3), CPU difficulty, and a Move List screen. The fighter card shows his stats and his four moves.
- **Marvel vs Capcom 3 layout:** □ light, △ medium, ◯ heavy, ✕ launcher. Chains L > M > H > S. The launcher pops him up; press up right after to super jump after him, then L, M, H in the air and ✕ spikes him to the floor (he bounces once). Crouching normals hit low, the crouching heavy is a sweep. Jumping normals from the air. Forward or back + heavy up close is a throw.
- **Defense:** hold back to block, down-back for lows, air block. Blockstun, pushback, chip damage on specials and hypers. Throws and command grabs go through a block. A knocked-down man is invincible until he gets up.
- **Specials:** QCF, QCB and dragon punch motions + a button; the button sets the strength (□ quick, △ normal, ◯ strong). L1 + □/△/◯ does the three specials with simple inputs, L1 + ✕ the hyper. Motion buffer in numpad notation relative to facing, 0.42 s window.
- **Hypers:** QCF + two attack buttons (pressed within 0.12 s of each other), one bar of meter (100; three bars max; built by hitting 1.5x, blocking 0.5x, being hit 0.8x damage). Flash, dark vignette, freeze, the name as a fire banner, then the move. Anything cancels into a hyper in its first 0.12 s; a normal that connected cancels into any special or a higher chain level.
- **Combos:** hit counter and damage shown on the attacker's side; scaling 7% a hit down to 30%. Hitstop scales with damage.
- **Move types** (src/e_fightmode.js FM_SP): fireball, rising uppercut (invincible start, launches, landing recovery), shoulder rush (knockdown), spin kick (4 hits travelling), teleport (behind him), ground pound (leap, slam, floor shockwave that hits grounded men), command grab, counter stance (eats a strike and answers it), lightning legs (6 hits), fadeaway (hop back, long strike), dive kick (air only). Hypers (FM_HY): beam across the stage, barrage (10-hit rush), slam (leap on him and crash down with a wave), quake (slam plus seven rocks from the sky), rising fury (5-hit triple uppercut), super grab (38).
- **Who gets what:** FM_SIG names signature sets for 26 stars (Curry: Splash / Step-back / Heat Check / Night Night; LeBron: Freight Train / Chase-Down counter / Tomahawk / The King Slam; Jordan: Baseline Drive / The Shrug / Air Jordan / Flu Game; Kobe: Fadeaway / Footwork legs / Mamba Rise / Mamba Mentality; Draymond: The Kick / DPOY Read / Box Out / Flagrant Two; Wemby, Jokić, KG, Rodman, Duncan quakes; Kawhi The Clamps; Kyrie Uncle Drew teleport and Ankle Breaker; Shump Shorty Legs and Shorty Special; Arenas Hibachi; Dirk One-Leg Fadeaway...). Everyone else gets an archetype from his ratings: big, shooter, dunker, lockdown, speedster, mid-range scorer.
- **CPU:** reads distance and what you are doing: blocks strikes it sees (crouching for lows), jumps fireballs and grabs, anti-airs jump-ins with his uppercut, finishes combos on a stunned man (ground chain into launcher into air chain into spike), cashes a hyper when he has a bar, throws up close, dashes and jumps in from mid range, zones with fireballs from far. Rookie / Pro / All-Star set reaction (0.5 / 0.32 / 0.18 s), block chance (30 / 55 / 82%), aggression and hyper use. On Rookie and Pro he flashes about 0.3 s before a special or a hyper (the same tell as the ref fight), so you can block.
- **Presentation:** side camera that slides with the pair, pulls back as they spread and lifts for air combos, zooms on hypers. Health bars with a red lag bar, round-win dots, 99 clock, meter bars with LV, combo counter, move names, ROUND n / FIGHT! / K.O. / PERFECT / TIME OVER / DRAW banners, winner pose, results screen with Rematch, Change Fighters, Main Menu. Options pauses (Resume, Move List, Restart, Change Fighters, Quit). Shorty calls the winner.
- Fighters wear their team's home kit (P1) or away kit (P2); free agents get red or white.

## Checks (headless)

- 8 CPU vs CPU matchups across all three difficulties: no errors, every round ended by K.O. in about 20-40 s, specials, throws, air combos and hypers all fired, longest combo 16 hits.
- Pad input test: QCF, QCB and DP motions register, the two-button hyper spends a bar, L1 simple inputs fire all four, the chain L M H S connects into the launcher and the super jump, a standing block eats a heavy and a sweep goes under it while a crouch block takes it.
- Bugs the pad test caught and fixed: presses made during hitstop were dropped (now buffered, like a real fighting game), two buttons in the same frame did not count as a hyper, the uppercut's landing recovery stuck to every later jump.
- A decent bot (blocks 60% of what it sees with a 0.15-0.25 s reaction, chains when close, specials at mid range, hypers on a stunned man) vs the CPU after the CPU learned to block on sight and punish recoveries: Rookie 4-0, Pro 2-4, All-Star 1-4. Rounds last about 20-35 s.
- Menus checked: Fight on the title, setup, move list, pause, Change Fighters (HUD and overlay hidden), Quit to Menu, and a Play Now game still starts clean afterwards. No console errors.
