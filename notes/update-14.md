# Version 14: the Fight mode goes cartoon 2D

Nutty asked (2026-10-19, after v13): make the fight mode a cartoony 2D mode, assign every basketball player a random fighter across all games, give them all fluid Street Fighter button inputs, ten unique moves each, and keep it playing like Marvel vs Capcom.

## What changed

- **Look:** the fight no longer uses the 3D models. A 2D canvas (`#fm2d`) replaces the Three.js view during a fight: rubber-hose cartoon fighters drawn from pose angles (jersey with the number, shorts, skin, hair, beard or headband, faces that go angry / hit / K.O. / grin / block), squash and stretch on landings and hits, comic-book effects (impact stars, POW! / WHAM! / BAM! text, sparks, dust, rings, hyper flash and vignette, speed lines on dashes). Three stages with parallax: THE GYM (bleachers with a bobbing crowd, HOOP DREAMS banners, lockers), BLACKTOP SUNSET (skyline, chain-link fence, setting sun), ROOFTOP NIGHT (moon, stars, lit windows). Stage picked from the two names plus the style seed. The camera slides, pulls back as they spread, lifts for air combos and zooms on hypers and K.O.s.
- **Random fighter styles:** eleven styles, each with ten unique named moves (two command normals, three motions x punch / kick = six specials, a level 1 hyper and a level 3 hyper). Every baller in every game is assigned a style by a hash of his id and the style seed, so the same baller keeps the same style until you press Reroll Styles on the Fight screen (CFG.fseed, saved). Styles: WORLD WARRIOR (shoto), SONIC SOLDIER (charge), IRON CYCLONE (grappler, 122% hp, slow), LIGHTNING LEGS, SHADOW STRIDER (ninja, teleports and dives), BERSERKER (beast), HEAVYWEIGHT (boxer, charge), BLOOD TALON (Tekken, no projectiles), GAMMA TITAN (130% hp, 120% power, slow), PSYCHO LORD (dictator, charge), VOID MAGE (zoner). Each has hp / speed / power multipliers. Move templates in FM_TPL (overhead, lunge, fireball, sonic, orb, arc, dp, flip, rush, spin, lariat, teleport, pound, grab, counter, legs, step, slide, dive, stomp; hypers beam, barrage, slam, quake, rising, super grab, crusher, storm, thousand kicks, rocks).
- **Street Fighter inputs:** motions are lenient (half-second window, loose corners: 2,6 counts as a quarter-circle, 6,2,6 as a dragon punch, half-circles count as their quarter-circle), charge styles hold back about 0.7 s then forward, or down then up, with a 0.3 s release grace; a button pressed early waits up to 0.22 s for the fighter to be free (so a special comes out the moment the previous move ends), presses during hitstop are buffered, an up pressed a touch early still super jumps after a launcher. Punch buttons (□ △) and kick buttons (◯ ✕) give different specials on the same motion. L1 + □ / △ / ◯ are the three punch specials, hold R2 too for the kick versions, L1 + ✕ the level 1 hyper, L1 + R2 + ✕ the level 3. Hypers: first motion + two attack buttons (one bar), second motion + two buttons (three bars, LV3 on the meter).
- **Still Marvel vs Capcom:** same layout and chains (□ △ ◯ ✕, L > M > H > S, launcher, super jump, air chain, spike), normals cancel into specials and anything cancels into a hyper in its first frames, hitstop, combo counter with damage scaling, blockstun, chip, pushback, throws, knockdown invincibility. Normals now step in a little and combo pushback is halved so chains stay in reach; the launcher reaches a touch further.
- **Menus:** the Fight screen's fighter card shows the style name, where it is from, hp / speed / power, and all ten moves with their inputs. Reroll Styles item. The Move List explains the inputs and lists every style with its ten moves. Results screen names the style and both hypers.
- Fight states no longer render the 3D scene (the canvas `#gl` is hidden); Play Now and Blacktop are untouched.

## Checks (headless, Playwright with the software canvas)

- CPU vs CPU, eight matchups covering nine of the eleven styles, all three difficulties: no errors, every round ended by K.O. in 9-27 s, every style's specials, command normals and hypers fired, longest combo 9.
- Pad input test on three styles (charge, ninja, shoto): all six specials fire from their motions with punch and kick buttons, both command normals, both hypers spend the right meter, a hyper without enough meter does nothing, every L1 simple input fires (an air-only move correctly refuses on the ground), the chain L M H S connects into the launcher, a super jump and air L M H + spike for an 8-hit combo at six different up / button timings, a press during hitstop comes out after it, a standing block eats a heavy, a crouch block takes a sweep but not an overhead.
- Bugs found and fixed on the way: a fighter hung in the air after a launch (air test off by a hundredth), the CPU never threw normals (its presses were not reaching the new input path), round wins were not counted, the super jump was overwritten by a plain jump, the launcher missed after a heavy's pushback, jersey numbers read mirrored when facing left, the gym crowd drew 400 arcs a frame (now only what is on screen, batched by colour).
- A decent bot vs the CPU, one round each, three matchups: Rookie 3-0, Pro 2-1, All-Star 1-2, rounds 20-30 s.
- Menu smoke: Fight screen items, Reroll Styles changes the seed and the card, pause, Move List from pause, Resume, Change Fighters (HUD and canvas hidden, 3D view back), Quit to Menu, then a Play Now game starts clean. Screenshots at 1280x720 reviewed for all three stages, a hyper, an air combo and a K.O.
