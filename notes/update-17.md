# Version 17: The Gym, fluid courts and independent players

Nutty asked (2026-10-19): "for the gym, make it a fluid transition to each court. and if i play this with another player, they can infinitely pick up their own balls to shoot and shoot on their own without the camera angles tailoring to 1 player".

- **Lanes**: every human in the gym has a lane: their own ball, basket, defender, rebounder, challenge, hint line and camera. The engine still has one live ball (`B`) and one possession, so each lane is swapped in with `gymUse(i)` / `gymWith(i, fn)` (fields in `GYM_BK`, plus `S.poss` and the attack directions). Each lane also swaps in its own `team.players` arrays (`L.pl`), so contests, blocks and AI only see that lane's people. `tryPickup` skips players of other lanes.
- **Split screen** with two players: one viewport per lane (scissor), a second PerspectiveCamera, a mirror pass per view, per-view particle size and backboard ghosting, a divider, per-lane banner/toast/hint (`.gl` overlays, `gymEl()` in d_game routes banner/toast/hint), name tags off, shot meters positioned per view (`toScreen(x,y,z,V)`). The second ball is its own mesh (`GYM.ball2`).
- **Fluid courts**: the 2K camera yaw eases (damp 2.8) and only swings to the other basket once you are 3.2 m past the middle (hysteresis). A held stick keeps its direction until released or reversed (`gymStickWorld`, `STICK.h` says whose stick), so a long run never turns you around. Cameras sit inside the walls and the ceiling is hidden unless the camera is under it. Starting a challenge no longer teleports: you jog to the first spot (`L.glide`) and the clock waits.
- Start spots: split P1 (-3.5, 6.4) facing -x, P2 (3.5, 6.4) facing +x. Setup label: "2 Players (split screen, own balls)". Challenge records are shared (saved on the device); each player's challenge, level and NPCs are their own.
- Also fixed: the toast was off-center (keyframe `rise` overrode `translateX(-50%)`; `#toast` now uses `rise2`).

Checks (headless, 2 mock pads): 5 of 5 simultaneous jumpers at opposite ends with each lane's own ball and basket, both players get balls from the racks at the same time with strays left behind, P1 spawns a defender while P2 runs a 3PT Contest (only P2's spot ring shows, P1's NPC never touches P2), cameras independent (P1 flips, P2 does not), 1-player run across the whole gym: no stick reversal, one smooth camera swing, glide max 0.11 m per frame with the clock waiting, pause, restart, Play Now and Blacktop clean, re-entering the gym repeatedly leaves no stray HUD. Not covered: real GPU frame rate in split screen (two scene renders plus two mirror passes per frame; Graphics Low turns the mirror off).

- v17b: the gym player pick now cycles all 49 players (it used to skip the default Blacktop-style opponent and teammate squads); only your own two are kept apart.
- v17c: the gym defender is no longer tied to one court: when he gets the ball he shoots at the end he is standing at (`L.npcHad` in gymLane); the camera only follows his lane's human.
- v17c looks: buildPlayer gained hair styles (afro, buzz, hightop, curly, cornrows, dreads, slicked, manbun, mohawk), extras are now space separated (stubble, stache, goatee, graybeard, beard, bigbeard, band, sleeve, brow), `skinC` (exact skin color), `bandC`, `sleeveC`. Only a few roster rows were changed, because web search returned headlines but no photo descriptions and image hosts are blocked here: Klay fade (May 2025 source), LeBron shaved head (Jul 2026 source, unverified), Harden full beard (Jul 2025 source); Bird white hair, Kerr gray hair, Dirk short hair, Kyrie goatee, Jokic beard are general knowledge, not source-checked.
